See it. Sort it. Learn why.

A phone camera identifies an everyday item, suggests how to sort it in Macau, and explains the reason — turning a bin decision into a learning moment.

The Problem

Recycling rules are local and depend on condition: clean paper can be recycled, a greasy box cannot, and a used tissue is never paper recycling. One wrong item can contaminate a whole bin — but students rarely see the reason behind the rule.

Our Solution

Take or upload a photo. EcoScan AI identifies the object, gives a cautious recycling category based on Macau guidance, and shows an English reason, cleaning steps, a learning fact, and a quick quiz.

The AI Method

  • Real vision model: DeepSeek deepseek-flash (image input supported).
  • One model identifies the item and writes the English reason and quiz.
  • A strict prompt applies only Macau guidance — no other region's rules.
  • Uncertain or unmatched items return unknown. It never guesses.
  • The API key stays on the server; images are processed in memory.

Why it is different

  • Built on Macau guidance, citing the DSPA.
  • Teaches the "why", not just the label.
  • Honest by design: shows uncertainty and flags unconfirmed rules.
  • Student-built and fully explainable.
